Steady Health. What can you do?

Version 2 of the app, in the approved design language, reorganised around what a person's body can do. Four abilities replace weight as the headline: Get up, Go, Carry, Steady. Weight stays as one of the levers. The person names what they want to be able to do in their own words and the on-device model turns those words into things the app can track. The phone times and counts the monthly check. Once there is enough history, the app offers to test one change at a time and reports the honest result. The whole spectrum is served: on your feet, with a walker or cane, in a wheelchair, or mostly in bed, each with its own version of Move. Same tokens, same shapes, same voice as version 1; version 1 is kept beside this file, untouched.

22SettingsOne new row at the top: how you get around, because it decides the version of the app. The rest holds from version 1.

What changed from version 1

The headline

Four abilities on Today instead of the weight hero. Weight is a small block and has its own page as a lever.

The third tab

Abilities replaces History. The path and the weight line live inside it.

The first question

How you get around. It produces four versions of the app: on your feet, walker or cane, wheelchair, mostly in bed.

Visible AI, three places

Your words become tracked abilities (screen 3). The phone times and counts the monthly check (10, 11). Try it and see runs a two-week test on you and reports honestly (14, 15). No chat anywhere.

Same is a result

Holding steady is reported as a win. Decline is described once, quietly, with everything that held beside it (13).

Unchanged

The tokens, the shapes, the voice, the daily three, the smoothed weight, no calories, no streaks, nothing red, pacing mode, exclusions in movement words.
