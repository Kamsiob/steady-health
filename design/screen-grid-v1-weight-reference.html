Steady. The full grid, in the look.

Twenty-three screens in the approved language: sunrise orange and deep navy, colour blocks that do the organising, illustration built from shapes, big consistent radii, Figtree at heavy weights. Every screen sits on the same 18-pixel grid with 12-pixel gaps, every card is 24 radius and every hero is 30, every glyph is 52 pixels. Every sentence was rewritten to pass one test: would a friend say this out loud. No untaught words. The system holds, and the day-one, week-one, month-one, month-six, and return logic from the research is all here.

Alignment rules every screen follows

Grid

18 px side padding. 12 px between blocks. 16 to 18 px inside blocks. Section headers sit 6 px above their block, never floating.

Radius

Hero 30. Cards and blocks 24. List items 22. Glyph tiles 16. Buttons and pills fully round. No other values exist.

Glyphs

Always 52 px in a card, 44 px tile with a 28 px glyph in a list. Every glyph is a circle, an arc, a path, or a bubble. No faces.

Type

Hero number 64, screen title 26, block value 24, item heading 15, body 14, caption 12. Nothing in between.

Colour meaning

Orange is the walk and the weight line. Navy is where you are and the primary action on dark. Green is done, only. Butter marks a milestone. Sand, sky, and green tints sort the three daily things and never change jobs.

Sky

The hero follows the clock on Today and on Welcome back. Everywhere else the hero is navy so it doesn't compete.
