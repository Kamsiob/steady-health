History

Evening. Same shapes, navy instead of orange, a moon instead of a sun, a few stars. All three cards are done and gone white. The bottom module swaps from the next walk to the last test. Nothing on the screen had to be invented for night.

The system behind it

Palette

Sunrise orange and deep navy carry the app. Sky, butter, sand, and two tints do the sorting. Green is only ever "done."

Shapes, not characters

Every illustration is built from circles, arcs, and hills. A sun, a moon, a dotted path, a speech bubble with two dots instead of a face. Warm without being cute.

Colour does the organising

Weighing is sand, talking is sky, moving is green. Once learned, you find them without reading. Done is white with a green mark, so the day visibly clears.

Type

Figtree, 800 and 900 for headings and the number, 600 for labels, 500 for body. Round terminals are what make it friendly; the weight is what keeps it from being soft.

Radius

30 on the hero, 24 on cards, 26 on the walk module, full round on buttons and pills. Big radii everywhere, consistently, is most of the Headspace feel.

Time of day

The hero changes with the clock: sun to moon, orange to navy. The rest of the screen stays put, so the change reads as weather, not as a new layout.
