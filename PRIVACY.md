# Privacy Policy: Steady Health by Kamsiob

Effective September 6, 2026.

Steady Health stores everything on your phone and nothing anywhere else.

## What the app collects
Nothing. Steady has no account, no server, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising. The information you enter, your weight, your check-ins, your walks, your photos if you choose to take them, your height and age if you choose to enter them, is written to an encrypted database on your device and never leaves it unless you export it yourself.

## What the app never does
It never sends your data anywhere. It never sells or shares anything, because it has nothing to sell or share. It never reads anything on your phone outside its own storage. It never contacts a server of ours, because there isn't one.

## Optional features that use the internet, and only when you turn them on
The optional on-device reader is a model file downloaded once from a stated source when you choose to add it. After the download it runs entirely on your phone. Nothing you enter is sent during or after the download.

Health Connect, if you turn it on, lets Steady read your weight, steps, and sleep from a watch or scale app already on your phone. Steady Health only reads; it never writes to Health Connect, and the data stays on your device.

## Permissions
Steady Health asks for a permission only at the moment a feature needs it and explains why in the request. Activity recognition for counting steps during a walk. Camera for counting repetitions during a check, and for Sunday photos if you use them. When the camera counts repetitions, the picture is looked at as it happens and never saved. No video or photo of you is written to storage during a check. Location only if you start a walk with distance tracking on. You can decline any of them and the app keeps working without that feature.

## Your data is yours
Export everything at any time from Settings as ordinary files: spreadsheets, your photos, and a one-page summary. Delete everything at any time from Settings. Deletion is immediate and complete, and there is no copy anywhere else to delete.

## Children
Steady Health is for adults and is not directed at anyone under eighteen.

## Changes
If this policy ever changes, the new version and its date will be published at the same address as this one and inside the app. The app will never begin collecting data as a change to this policy; that would be a different app.
