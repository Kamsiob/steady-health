# The clinician handout

ADDENDUM-03 Part 19 and MASTER_SPEC.md section 12. One page, printable, for a
clinician to hand somebody at the end of an appointment.

It leads with the home programme, because that is the reason it works. Most people
handed a home programme never do it, and the app's claim on a physiotherapist's
attention is not that it is a good exercise app. It is that the sheet they just
printed stops being a sheet in a drawer.

Two page sizes, A4 and US Letter, same content, different margins. Black and white
only: no grey fills, no reversed type, no colour that carries meaning, nothing under
9 point. It has to survive a toner-low office laser printed at "draft".

## What is deliberately not on it

**No logo above the heading.** The first thing on the page is what the person gets.
A brand mark at the top of a handout is the page saying who it is from before it says
what it is for, and the clinician handing it over has already said who it is from.

**No feature list.** Four lines is four lines. Every extra one is read by fewer
people, and the fifth would have been about weight or about Progress, neither of which
is why a therapist would hand this to anybody.

**No claims about outcomes.** Not that it improves adherence, not that it reduces
anything, not a percentage. The app has no trial behind it and a handout that implied
one would be the app doing exactly what its own reading validator exists to stop.

**No mention of falling, risk, decline, or age.** The person reading this is standing
in front of their therapist holding a sheet. The register is the same as the rest of
the app.

**No tear-off slip, no "ask your therapist about", no second page.** One page that a
clinician can print fifty of.

**The three ruled lines are the point of the bottom third.** A handout a clinician
writes on is a handout that gets taken home. They are wide enough for a movement name
and a number in ordinary handwriting, which is why there are three of them and not six
smaller ones.

## Production notes

- A4: 20 mm margins all round. US Letter: 0.75 inch. Same content, no reflow beyond
  the margin change, so the two files are proof-read once.
- Body 11 point, heading 20 point, the strip 10 point. Serif or sans, one family.
- The QR code encodes the Play listing URL as plain text, error correction level M,
  and prints at 25 mm with its quiet zone. It is tested by scanning the printed page,
  not the screen.
- Black ink only. If a printer renders the ruled lines faintly they are still ruled
  lines; if it renders a grey fill faintly the text on it is gone, which is why there
  are no grey fills.
- Both files are generated from this document rather than authored separately, so the
  copy cannot drift between them.
