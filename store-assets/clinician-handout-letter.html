The home programme they just gave you, in an app that runs it.

Photograph the sheet. The app reads it and asks you to check every line before it saves anything. Then it runs that programme, exactly as it was given: it does not add to it, take from it, or change the numbers.

It leads you through, out loud, so you can put the phone on a table and do the movements rather than read them.

It keeps track of what you actually did, and makes a one page summary to bring back to your next appointment.

Free. No account, no sign up, no ads, nothing to buy. Everything stays on your phone and nothing is sent anywhere, including to us.
